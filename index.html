About Wurks

CARS CAME FIRST.

Long before Wurks Auto Sales became a dealership, cars were already a major part of our lives. Years were spent working on them, rebuilding them, modifying them, learning their strengths and weaknesses, and enjoying them with friends and family who share the same passion.

Our passion for cars grew during the 1990s and early 2000s, a period that brought some of the most memorable innovation, performance, and personality the automotive world had seen. The enthusiast scene was expanding quickly, and we grew up right alongside it. Cars became projects, late nights in the garage, weekend drives, road trips, friendships, and a shared appreciation for the culture surrounding them.

The more time we spent around cars, the more we wanted to understand what was happening beneath the hood and how everything worked together. Hands-on experience eventually grew into formal automotive training and years of learning how to diagnose, repair, maintain, and better understand the vehicles we enjoy.

Starting Wurks Auto Sales felt like a natural next step—an opportunity to take years of automotive experience and turn it into a small business rooted in the same passion that started it all.

Wurks Auto Sales didn’t create our passion for cars—it grew from it.
